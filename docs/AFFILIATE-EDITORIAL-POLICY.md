# Affiliate and editorial policy

- The importer stores normal official product URLs only.
- An approved affiliate URL is added manually in the Recommended Tool record.
- Affiliate relationships are disclosed on the tools page and near marked article links.
- Marked affiliate links receive `sponsored nofollow noopener`.
- A recommendation should survive the end of the affiliate program.
- Pricing, availability and product terms must be verified before publishing time-sensitive claims.
- Sponsored placement never creates a fake rating, testimonial or engineering result.
