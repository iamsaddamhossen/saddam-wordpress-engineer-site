# Content Accuracy Policy

Accuracy is more valuable than aggressive marketing.

## Allowed claims

- A capability demonstrated in the repository.
- A responsibility Saddam Hossen can explain in detail.
- A public project that can be opened and reviewed.
- An NDA-safe summary that separates individual responsibility from team ownership.
- A metric supported by analytics, monitoring, a client record or a reproducible benchmark.

## Avoid

- Invented testimonials or unverifiable anonymized quotations.
- Approximate numbers presented as exact.
- Claiming sole ownership of team work.
- Calling a proof of concept production-ready.
- Publishing client code, credentials, private URLs or personal data.
- Repeating generic paragraphs only to make an article longer.

## Explanation rule

Every public statement should survive two checks: “Where is this implemented?” and “What exactly did you do, choose or verify?”
